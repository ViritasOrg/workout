Warning triangle on the car: Polestar did not answer

What it means

The app asked your Polestar to start or stop climate, and the car refused or gave no answer within 90 seconds. The yellow triangle on the car in the header stays until a later try works or you tap OK on the message.

What happens if you ignore it

The car is probably not heating or cooling as you asked. If it was a stop that failed, climate may still be running and stops by itself after 30 minutes.

How to fix it

  1. Tap the car in the header. The message shows the reason the car or Polestar gave.
  2. Tap Try again. A car with weak mobile coverage, or one that has been parked for long, often answers the second time.
  3. If it keeps failing, open the Polestar app on your phone and check that the car is online there and that climate can be started from it.
  4. If the message says your Polestar ID was not accepted, go to Settings → Polestar in this app and enter it again.

Home equipment: the weight you typed was not added

What it means

Under Plan → Home equipment you can add a kettlebell, dumbbell or plate weight that is not in the list. The box only takes a number of kilograms between 0.1 and 200, such as 24 or 2.5 (a comma works too). Anything else, or an empty box, is not added.

What happens if you ignore it

Nothing breaks. That weight is simply not in your list of weights, and the weights you already picked stay as they were.

How to fix it

  1. Tap the box under the weights, type only the number of kg, without "kg" after it.
  2. Tap Add. The weight appears in the list, ticked.
  3. To remove a weight you added, untick it.
